How This Works

Work through the Checklist tab phase by phase, top to bottom — it's built in the order that actually makes sense (pick your sponsor, build your brand, quietly get your ducks in a row, THEN notify your current broker and pull the trigger). Check items off as you go; your progress saves automatically right in this file, in your own browser, and will still be there next time you open it.

Use the Your Tracking Sheet tab to log your sponsor/co-sponsor info, the details you'll need for your application, and every platform/login you touch along the way. You can download it as a CSV any time and drop it straight into Google Sheets.

Rule #1, no matter what: you cannot conduct business under eXp — no advertising, no showings, no offers — until your license shows ACTIVE with your state's licensing authority. No exceptions.

On sponsors and co-sponsors: you get ONE chance to name both, and the window to add a co-sponsor is short and closes fast. Don't sit on this decision — get it locked in first, before anything else.

Keep your sponsor/co-sponsor posted: update them as you move through each phase so they know exactly where you're at — don't wait until you're stuck to loop them in.
